# The Amethyst Residence

A stunning modern home nestled in the Laurentian hills, The Amethyst Residence represents the pinnacle of our residential design philosophy. Every element — from the rift-sawn walnut paneling to the custom brass hardware — was designed and fabricated in our Mississauga studio.

## Design Approach

The clients approached us with a clear vision: a home that feels warm and grounded despite its contemporary aesthetic. We responded with a material palette dominated by natural walnut, honed limestone, and blackened steel — materials that age gracefully and develop character over time.

The custom millwork throughout the home creates a sense of continuity, with consistent grain direction and shadow gaps that read as a single unified gesture rather than individual pieces. Floor-to-ceiling glass panels frame views of the surrounding maple forest, blurring the boundary between interior and landscape.
